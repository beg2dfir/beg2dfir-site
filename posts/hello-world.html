Beg2DFIR here.

Beg2DFIR: Beginner to Digital Forensic and Incident Response.

Even though I feel like a beginner, this is not exactly ex nihilo — not from nothing.

Somewhere between old games, cheat codes, and movies like The Matrix, computers started feeling less like boxes and more like worlds I wanted to understand.

My first real doorway into the field was remote help desk work. Since then, I have touched different parts of tech through work, study, troubleshooting, and small experiments. The pieces were real, but they were scattered. Beg2DFIR is where I start organizing them into a deliberate path.

Beg2DFIR is a place to organize what I’m learning and building as I go. Some of it will be notes. Some of it will be study tools, projects, videos, or small experiments. The point is to make the process useful, visible, and easier to follow.

I want this to feel beginner-friendly without being watered down. Clear enough to follow, practical enough to use, and honest enough to show the work while it is still taking shape.

Over time, this may grow into apps, games, tools, videos, and resources I cannot fully see yet. For now, it starts here.

Welcome to Beg2DFIR.
