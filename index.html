The B2D Byte

The B2D Byte will appear alongside study content showing what a topic might contain.

More theoretical content will look like 1111000, while more hands-on content will look like 00001111

  • Bit 1 - Wordsearches - Become familiar with the language of the topic
  • Bit 2 - Flash Cards - Definitions are paired to keywords
  • Bit 3 - Crosswords - Practice keywords, given the definitions
  • Bit 4 - Quantitative Layer - Math based problems to tie concepts together
  • Bit 5 - PlayerBook - Common repeatable processes
  • Bit 6 - Model - Apply Theory to build scenarios and predict outcomes
  • Bit 7 - Lab - Simulate model or scenario
  • Bit 8 - Investigate - Explain anomolies from the lab
